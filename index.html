About me

I'm a student, and I'm an introvert. You won't see my face in the videos. You'll see my hands, my desk, my bike, and the tracker filling up.

The name comes from two places. Lazy Genius is my gamer tag. Arching Pegasus was an old name I never understood, until this series became an arc. A pegasus that would rather sleep in, but flies anyway. That's the whole channel.

The videos are simple on purpose. A day card, quick clips of the routine, a flash of the study timelapse, and an end card with today's hours and the running total. Same shape every day, so the only thing that changes is the number.

No fake productivity. If I miss a day, the next video says so and the counter keeps going.

Rules of the arc

  • One video a day

    Posted every evening. Short, same structure, no exceptions.

  • Three hours minimum

    Study is filmed as a timelapse. If it isn't on camera, it doesn't count.

  • The counter never resets

    A missed day is posted as a missed day. Day numbers keep going.

  • Games are a reward

    One hour of Valorant after the study block, never before.

  • Bed by midnight

    6:30 alarm means the day has to end. Sleep is part of the routine.

  • No face, no excuses

    Hands, desk, bike, tracker. The work is the content.
